The installation list

Open Installations. Everything below happens instantly, without reloading the page:

  • Search by name, owner or address in Search installations….
  • Filter by type (All Types), status (All Statuses) and installation date range.
  • Sort by newest or oldest, location A–Z or Z–A, or capacity.

The line above the table says Showing N of M installations, so you always know whether a filter is hiding records. The row icons edit an installation (and, for administrators, delete it).

Add an installation

Click + Add Installation on the list, or click an empty spot on the dashboard map to start with that point already chosen.

  1. Set the location Click the map on the form to drop the pin, and drag it to adjust. Latitude and longitude fill in, and so do the address, barangay, municipality and province, looked up from the point. Anything you type in those fields yourself is kept. You can also type the coordinates directly.
  2. Choose the owner In Owner, pick the client's account if they have one. That lets them see the installation and report problems. Otherwise choose Owner has no account yet… and type their name; you can link the account later.
  3. Fill in the details Installation Name (e.g. "Dela Cruz Residence"), type, capacity in kWp, installation date, status and any notes. Fields marked * are required.
  4. Add photos (optional) JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each.
  5. Save The installation appears in the list and on the map at once. If something is wrong, the form says what and keeps everything you typed.

Coordinates

  • Latitude comes first, then longitude, in decimal degrees: 16.6870, 121.5480. The map's pin is the easiest way to get them right.
  • From Google Maps: right-click the spot and click the coordinates to copy them.
  • Points outside the configured map area are refused.

Edit an installation

Use the edit icon on the list, or Edit details on the installation's card on the map. Change what you need and save. Every change is recorded in the audit trail with the old and new values. You can add or remove photos here too.

Deleting Only administrators can delete an installation. If a site is retired, set its status to Decommissioned instead; it stays in the history.

Import and export

Bring in many installations from a spreadsheet, or take the list out as CSV, GeoJSON or PDF. See Import and export.

Client Desk: inquiries and reports

For administrators this menu is called Administration; it has the same two pages.

Inquiries

Messages sent through Contact Us on the public home page, from people who are not signed in. Each one is also emailed to the company inbox.

  1. Open Inquiries and click an inquiry to see the message and contact details.
  2. Call or email them back.
  3. Update the status: New → Contacted → Closed.

Client reports

Problems clients report on their own installations: the system not producing power, physical damage, a maintenance or cleaning request, monitoring not updating, or something else.

  1. Open Client Reports and click a report. You see what the client said, who reported it, the installation, and the full history.
  2. Add an update: choose a status (Open, In progress, Resolved) and write a note, e.g. "Technician visiting on Thursday morning to check the inverter." Then click Add update.
  3. Internal notes: tick Internal note for something only staff should see. The client is notified of status changes and of notes that are not internal.
Maintenance hold When a maintenance report moves to In progress, an operational or inactive installation changes to Under maintenance automatically. When the report is resolved, the previous status comes back, unless another maintenance report on the same site is still in progress, or someone changed the status by hand in the meantime.
