GIS-Based Solar Installation Tracking System

SolarCORE puts every solar installation a company has built on one interactive map, with its details, photos and status. It turns those records into analytics, reports and exports, and gives clients a place to see their own systems and report problems.

What is SolarCORE?

Solar installation companies often keep their records in spreadsheets, paper files and chat threads. Answering simple questions then takes real effort: how many systems are installed in a town, how much capacity is running, or which sites are close to a new customer.

SolarCORE keeps those records in one database with real geographic coordinates. Every installation appears on a map, and the same records drive:

  • the dashboard: a map plus a summary of totals by type and status;
  • analytics by type, status, area and month, exportable to PDF;
  • exports to CSV, GeoJSON (for GIS software such as QGIS) and PDF;
  • client accounts, so owners can see their own systems and report problems;
  • an AI assistant that answers questions about the records in plain language.

It was built around the real operations of ABS Global Management, Inc., a solar installation company in Santiago City, Isabela, as a capstone project of the College of Information Technology, University of La Salette, Inc.

How it works

  • One source of truth. Installations live in PostgreSQL. The PostGIS extension stores each location as a geographic point, so distances and nearby searches are measured, not estimated.
  • A browser is all users need. SolarCORE is a website. It works on a computer, tablet or phone.
  • Access is decided on the server. Each page and API checks the user's role. Clients are limited to their own installations in every page, export, photo and assistant answer.
  • Everything important is recorded. Changes to installations and accounts, exports and imports go into an audit trail.
