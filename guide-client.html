User guide

Client guide

For owners of a solar installation. Your account shows your own installations, and nobody else's, and lets you report a problem directly to the company.

Create an account

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose Create account Enter your first and last name, a username, your email address and a password (at least 8 characters), then accept the terms. You can also sign up with Continue with Google.
  2. Wait for approval The company checks that you are a customer and approves your account. Until then, signing in says "Your account is awaiting administrator approval."
  3. Your installations are linked The company links your installations to your account. If you sign in and see none, contact them.

Signing in

  1. Enter your username and password on the sign-in page, or use Continue with Google.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email. It works for 10 minutes. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder, then request a new code after 60 seconds.

Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. After 30 minutes without activity you are signed out automatically; a warning lets you click Stay signed in.

Report a problem

  1. Open My Installations and go to Report a problem.
  2. Choose the installation and what kind of problem it is:
    • System not producing power
    • Physical damage
    • Maintenance or cleaning request
    • Monitoring / app not updating
    • Something else
  3. Describe it in Tell us what is happening, and leave a contact number if the company should call you.
  4. Click Send report. The company is notified straight away.

Follow a report

Your reports are listed under Your report history on My Installations. Open one to see its full history.

StatusMeaning
OpenReceived; not picked up yet.
In progressSomeone is working on it.
ResolvedThe company considers it fixed.

You are notified when the status changes or the company adds a note. To add information, such as "The red light came back this morning", use Add a follow-up on the report.

Ask the assistant

The chat button at the bottom right opens the SolarCORE Assistant. Try:

  • "How many installations do I have?"
  • "What is the total capacity of my installations?"
  • "What does hybrid mean?"

It answers about your own installations only. See AI assistant.

Not a client yet?

Use Contact Us on the SolarCORE home page to send the company a message. You do not need an account. The Public Map on the home page shows the areas where the company has installed systems.
