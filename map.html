Using SolarCORE

The map

The dashboard is a map of the installations you are allowed to see: all of them for staff, your own for a client. A summary panel sits on the left, and a column of tools sits beside it.

The summary panel

The panel on the left shows:

  • totals: installations, installed kWp, operational and under construction;
  • a By type breakdown, which doubles as the legend for the pin colours;
  • a By status breakdown;
  • quick actions, for staff.

Collapse it with the arrow on its edge to see more of the map. Clicking a pin opens that installation's details in the same panel; the back arrow returns to the summary.

Map tools

FiltersType and status filters, and for staff the map layers. A dot on the button means a filter is on.

Filters and layers

Open Filters:

  • Type and Status limit which installations are shown. The panel says Showing N of M installations, and so does the button's tooltip. Reset clears both filters.
  • Map layers (staff) change how the installations are drawn, not which ones:
    LayerShows
    PinsOne marker per installation, coloured by type.
    ClusterGroups nearby pins into one bubble with a count. Zoom in or click it to split it.
    HeatmapDensity: where installations are concentrated.
    CoverageA circle around each installation. Overlaps show the areas the company already serves.

The layers follow the filters: filter to Hybrid and the heatmap shows only hybrid systems.

Installation details

Click a pin to open its card: name, owner, address, type, capacity, status, date and photo.

  • Staff see Edit details, which opens the installation's form.
  • Staff can search for nearby installations from the card (below).

Nearby installations (proximity search)

For staff. With an installation's card open:

  1. Choose a radius: 500 m, 1 km, 2 km (the default), 5 km or 10 km.
  2. Click Find nearby. Installations within that distance are listed nearest first, with their distance, and the search circle is drawn on the map.

Distances are calculated by PostGIS on the earth's actual shape, not estimated on the flat map. Changing the radius re-runs the search.

Adding an installation from the map

Staff can click any empty spot on the map. SolarCORE offers to add an installation there, and opens the form with that point already set. See Add an installation.

The public map

Visitors who are not signed in can open Public Map from the home page. It shows where the company works, without revealing who:

  • only operational installations;
  • drawn as a heatmap of roughly 1 km squares, never an installation's own point;
  • zoom is capped so a square never looks like a particular house;
  • no names, owners or addresses, plus headline totals.

The map's home area

The map opens on the Philippines by default and does not pan far outside the configured area. Administrators of the server can change the starting point and area in config/geo.php; see Configuration → Map area.

The map needs the internet The map's background images come from Esri and OpenStreetMap. Without a connection, SolarCORE shows a message instead of a blank map. Your installation data still loads, and the list, analytics and form keep working; on the form you can type coordinates instead.
