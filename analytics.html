Analytics and notifications

The Analytics tab turns the installation records into figures and charts. Staff see the whole company; a client sees only their own installations.

Export to PDF

Click Export at the top of the page. The PDF contains the same charts plus tables of the exact numbers, the date it was made, and (for a client) a note that it covers their own installations only. Analytics exports are recorded in the audit trail.

Why "installed capacity" can be smaller than the sum of all systems An awarded or under-construction system is not producing anything yet. Counting it would overstate what is actually installed, so SolarCORE leaves it out of installed capacity everywhere: the dashboard, analytics, PDF exports and the assistant.

Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows a red count of unread notifications. Click it to see them; click one to open what it is about. You can also mark them all as read.

You areYou are notified when
Administratorsomeone registers and waits for approval; an installation is added, changed or deleted; plus everything a technician is notified of.
Techniciana client report arrives, changes status or gets a follow-up; an inquiry arrives; your own role is changed.
Clientyour account is approved; an installation is linked to your account; your report's status changes or the company adds a note to it.

You are not notified about your own actions.

Email

Besides login codes, SolarCORE sends:

  • to the company inbox (the address set as MAIL_FROM_ADDRESS): every Contact Us inquiry, every new client report and follow-up, and every new registration waiting for approval;
  • to clients: account approved or declined, an installation linked to their account, and notes on their reports.

Everything is also saved inside SolarCORE, so nothing is lost if an email does not arrive.
