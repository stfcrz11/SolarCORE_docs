Using SolarCORE

Help and settings

Every signed-in page has a way back to this guide, a Settings page for display and accessibility, and a short walkthrough the first time you open it.

The Help menu

WhereHow to open it
ComputerClick your name at the top right. The menu starts with Help.
Tablet or phoneTap the ⓘ button at the top right, next to the bell.
  • User Guide opens the guide for your role on this site, in a new tab: the Administrator, Technician or Client guide. The other guides stay readable too; what each role can do is enforced by SolarCORE itself, not by hiding documentation.
  • Terms & Privacy Client opens the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Notice you agreed to when you signed up, read-only, with tabs to jump between the two. Staff accounts are created by an administrator and never agree to these, so staff do not see this item.

Settings

Open the menu under your name and choose Settings (on a phone or tablet: ☰ → Settings, near the bottom). Every control applies as you click; there is no Save button.

SettingWhat it does
Dark ModeLight, Dark or Device (follows your computer or phone). Maps and photos keep their real colours.
High ContrastDarker text and stronger borders.
Text SizeFour sizes, from A− to A++, across all of SolarCORE.
Screen ReaderReads aloud what you move to with Tab and any text you select. Alt+Shift+R reads the whole page; Esc stops. Set the speed and try it with Test voice. Works in browsers that can read aloud, such as Chrome, Edge and Safari.
Reduce MotionTurns off animations and sliding effects.
Underline LinksLinks are underlined, so they can be told apart without relying on colour.
Strong Focus OutlineA thick blue outline shows where you are when using the keyboard.
Show SolShows or hides the assistant's button in the bottom-right corner. See Sol, the assistant.
Saved on this browser Settings are kept by the browser you set them in, not by your account. On another computer or phone, set them again. Reset to defaults at the bottom of the page puts everything back.

The first-time walkthrough

The first time an account opens a page, a short guided tour highlights what is on it, one step at a time. Some steps ask you to click the highlighted control, and a click that opens another page carries the tour there. On a phone or tablet, the tour opens the ☰ menu for you when a step is inside it.

  • Each page has its own tour, shown once: the dashboard, the installation list and form, import, analytics, Users, Audit Trail, Inquiries, Client Reports, My Installations, a single report, and Settings.
  • The dashboard tour introduces the map, the summary, search, filters, the bell, Sol, and where Help and Settings are.
  • Skip tour, the ✕ or Esc ends the walkthrough on every page for good.
