Using SolarCORE

AI assistant

The SolarCORE Assistant answers questions about the installation records in plain language. Open it with the chat button at the bottom right of any page after signing in.

What you can ask

Kind of questionExamples
Counts"How many installations do we have?" · "How many hybrid systems are in Santiago City?"
Capacity"What is the total capacity?" · "How many kWp are operational in Isabela?"
Lists"Show the installations in Barangay Villasis." · "Which systems are under maintenance?"

When you first open it, suggested questions appear as buttons. The conversation stays while you move between pages, until you sign out.

Why its numbers can be trusted

Language models are good with words and bad with numbers. They can state a wrong figure with confidence. SolarCORE therefore splits the work:

  1. Understand. The model works out what you are asking: a count, a capacity or a list, and for which place, type or status.
  2. Query. SolarCORE runs a normal database query for the figures. Place names are matched against places that really exist in the records, so a misspelt or invented place is reported as unknown rather than answered with a confident zero.
  3. Word. The model turns those figures into a sentence. It is given the numbers; it does not calculate them.

Installed capacity follows the same rule as everywhere else: systems that are awarded or under construction are not counted as installed.

Who sees what

  • Staff get figures for the whole company.
  • Clients get figures for their own installations only. Their account decides this, not their question, so asking "show me all installations" does not widen it. If a client asks about a place where they have no installations, the answer is that they have none there.
  • Questions about which sites are near each other are answered for staff only.
  • Owner names are never sent to the AI service for a client's question.
  • Questions unrelated to SolarCORE are politely declined.

When the AI service is off

The assistant keeps working without the AI service: if it is switched off (AI_ENABLED=false), has no key, or cannot be reached. It falls back to built-in rules that recognise the common questions, and the replies are plainer but the figures are the same. See Configuration → AI assistant.

Limits

  • It reads the records; it cannot add, change or delete anything.
  • Lists show a handful of installations at a time. For a full list, use the Installations page and its filters.
  • It knows only what is in SolarCORE, not, for example, live energy production.
